My Favourite Place

Alappuzha

Alappuzha is one of the most beautiful places in Kerala. It is famous for its its canals, lagoons, and calm waterways, it is often nicknamed the "Venice of the East".

Alappuzha is a wonderful place to enjoy nature and spend time with family and friends. The beautiful scenery and peaceful atmosphere make it my favourite place.
